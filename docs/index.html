Computer Vision & Biometric Pipeline Architecture

Engineered with Viola-Jones Haar Cascades, CLAHE illumination correction, and Local Binary Patterns Histograms (LBPH).

1

Haar Cascade Detection + CLAHE

Computes Integral Image sums $O(1)$ across multi-scale attentional cascades. Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization suppresses illumination variances and harsh glare.

2

Multimodal Liveness Check

Combines Laplacian focus variance ($\nabla^2 f$), ocular cascade tracking, and YCrCb chrominance clustering to reject printed paper photos and digital smartphone replay attacks.

3

LBPH Texture Classification

Divides normalized $200 \times 200$ face crops into $8 \times 8$ local grid cells, calculating circular binary codes. Matches against enrolled subjects using Chi-Square Distance metric.
