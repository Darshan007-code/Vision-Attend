Technical Architecture & Academic Defense

Why LBPH over Eigenfaces & Fisherfaces?

Eigenfaces (PCA) and Fisherfaces (LDA) are holistic and extremely sensitive to lighting and head pose variations. LBPH represents local micro-texture features independently across spatial cells, making it virtually invariant to illumination shifts.

How is Distance converted to Confidence?

In OpenCV LBPH, distance = 0 implies identical match. Our pipeline normalizes the Chi-Square distance against the threshold $(T=75.0)$: $Confidence = \max(0, (1 - D/T) \times 100\%)$.

Anti-Spoofing Architecture

Multimodal verification combining Haar eye-blink detection, Laplacian focus variance (screens yield abnormally low variance), and YCrCb chrominance skin cluster validation.
